About Me

I'm Johnlene Lorenz R. Nael. I spent years unsure of where I was going, dreaming of different paths, until I realized I had always been designing in my own quiet way. From coloring pages to arranging digital layouts, creating has always felt natural to me.

I may not paint on canvas, but I design experiences such as focusing on colors, structure, and making technology feel simple and human. I'm still learning, still exploring, and growing through every project, one design at a time.
